# Does AI Need to Be Human at All?

Interactive poster by Marlus Araujo and Wenjue Yang. The Future of Art in the Age of AI, HKUST (Guangzhou), 2026.

Live: https://archive.marlus.com/does-ai-need-to-be-human/

Twelve chapters, narrated by two agents: A writes, B draws. The images are made of particles in WebGL and respond to touch. It opens with VISIONS by Orkhan Mammadov and passes through Reveries of the Apparatus (Devaneios do Aparato) by Marlus Araujo.

Readings: Lev Manovich and Emanuele Arielli, *Artificial Aesthetics*; Benjamin Bratton, Anna Greenspan, Amy Ireland and Bogna Konior (eds.), *Machine Decision Is Not Final*.

## Use

Made for a touch screen or a projector. Portrait (9:16) and landscape layouts.

- Arrows or space: next and previous chapter
- A: auto play
- L: switch layout
- F: fullscreen
- `#visions`, `#reveries`, `#open` and the other chapter ids open a chapter directly

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole poster, one file with no build step. Settings are at the top of the script (`MEDIA`, `REVERIES_LINES`, `CONFIG`).
- `media/`: photographs of Reveries of the Apparatus.
- `fonts/`: Space Grotesk, IBM Plex Mono and a four-character subset of Noto Serif SC, self-hosted so the page works without Google Fonts. All under the SIL Open Font License.
